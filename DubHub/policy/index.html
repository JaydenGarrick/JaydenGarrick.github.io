Dub is an app for dubbing scenes: pick a pack, record your lines over the video, share the result with friends. Packs in the Download tab are made by people like you. This page is the deal everyone agrees to inside the app before publishing, downloading, or joining a session.

01Community guidelines

Share only what's yours to share — your own video, or content you hold the rights to. Published packs are reviewed by a human before anyone can download them. No harassment, no hate, nothing illegal. Copyright complaints are acted on, and repeat infringers lose publishing.

  • Rights first. A pack you publish must be your own footage, or material you have permission to distribute. Publishing includes an explicit affirmation of this.
  • People first. Nothing that harasses, degrades, or targets a real person; no hate; nothing sexualizing minors; nothing illegal to distribute.
  • Reviewed before shared. Nothing you publish reaches the public Download tab until it has been reviewed and approved — and a re-published new version goes back into review.
  • You're in control of what you see. Any pack can be reported from inside the app, and any creator can be blocked — their packs leave your device's catalog for good.

02Publishing a pack

When you publish, you affirm that you made the video or hold the rights to share it. That affirmation is recorded with the pack.

By publishing, you grant Dub a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, review, and distribute your pack to other Dub users — that's what makes the Download tab and session invites work. You keep ownership of your content. The license for a pack ends when it's removed from the catalog, except for copies other users already downloaded.

To remove a pack you published, or one you appear in, contact us.

03Copyright & takedowns

If you believe a pack on Dub infringes your copyright, tell us — from the app (Download → long-press the pack → Report… → Copyright concern, which lets you attach a reply address), or by email.

A complete takedown notice includes:

  1. The copyrighted work you say is infringed;
  2. The pack that infringes it — its title, and ideally its pack id (shown under the title in the app);
  3. Your name and a way to reach you;
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner;
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

What we do: we act on complete notices promptly — removed packs leave the Download tab and can no longer be shared through session invites. If you published the pack and believe the removal was a mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter-notice to the same address with the same information plus your consent to the jurisdiction of your federal district court (or ours, if you're outside the US).

Repeat infringers have their publishing identity banned: every pack they published is removed from the catalog, and future ones stay out.

04Privacy

Dub has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. This section is the app's privacy policy.

  • Your identity in Dub is a display name and avatar you choose, plus a signing key created on your device. Publishing and sessions use your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit; we never see your Apple ID, email, or password.
  • Recordings stay local. Your dubs live on your device. They leave it only when you export them or share them — to a session's invitees, or as a file you send.
  • Published packs are public. Publishing puts the pack, its title, your display name, and your publishing identity in the app's shared public database.
  • Reports are stored in that same public database — including, for copyright reports, the reply address you optionally provide. Don't put anything in a report you wouldn't want visible to other users of the app.
  • Blocks are yours alone. Blocking a creator or reporting-to-hide a pack is stored on your device and never leaves it.
  • Deletion: deleting the app removes everything local. To remove a pack you published from the shared catalog, contact us.
